Results for P08: Missing annotations. Minor — 84 cases
This pitfall consists in creating an ontology element and failing to provide human readable annotations attached to it. Results for P19: Defining multiple domains or ranges in properties. Critical — 2 cases
The domain or range (or both) of a property is defined by stating more than one rdfs:domain or rdfs:range statement. In OWL, multiple rdfs:domain or rdfs:range axioms are interpreted as conjunction (owl:intersectionOf).

• This pitfall appears in the following elements:
  › http://www.example.org/ontology/hospital#hasEmployee
  › http://www.example.org/ontology/hospital#hasDegree

Results for P38: No OWL ontology declaration. Important — Ontology-level
The owl:Ontology tag is not declared. This tag provides ontology metadata such as version information, license, provenance, and creation date.

Results for P39: Ambiguous namespace. Critical — Ontology-level
Neither the ontology URI nor the xml:base namespace is declared. The ontology namespace defaults to the file location, which is not stable.
